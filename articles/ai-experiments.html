AI Experiments

Hands-on experiments with new AI models, built with Playwright and TypeScript and measured honestly against plain-code baselines — what the model is actually good at, where it breaks, and what it costs.

Each article is also published on Medium.

01 Teaching a Decision Model to Play 2048 — and Why the Facts Mattered More Than the Model Jev, from TypeSafe AI, is a new kind of model: it never writes text, it only makes judgments. You give it a small state and a typed question, and about 230 ms later it hands back an answer with probabilities. I wanted to know what that is actually good for, so I made it play 2048 in a real browser and measured it against plain-code bots. The surprise wasn't how smart it was. It was how completely its play depended on the facts I chose to give it.
